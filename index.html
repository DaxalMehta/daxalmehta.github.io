Understanding the Growth of Black Holes Across Cosmic Time

My research focuses on the formation and growth of black holes in the early Universe using high-resolution cosmological simulations. I develop numerical models that connect black hole accretion, feedback, stellar evolution, and galaxy formation to understand how supermassive black holes emerge only a few hundred million years after the Big Bang.

Black Hole Growth

Exploring how stellar-mass black hole seeds evolve into supermassive black holes using cosmological simulations.

Hierarchical Mergers

Investigating black hole mergers in nuclear star clusters and their implications for gravitational-wave observations.

Simulation Development

Developing sub-grid models, dynamical friction prescriptions, feedback routines, and sink particle algorithms in cosmological simulations.
